Frequently asked questions

What exactly does the Paragraph Counter define as a 'paragraph'?add

Our Paragraph Counter typically defines a paragraph as a block of text separated from other text blocks by one or more blank lines (i.e., double line breaks or more). A single line break usually signifies a new line within the same paragraph, while multiple line breaks signify a new paragraph.

Is the Paragraph Counter truly free and repeated to use?add

Yes, absolutely! Our Paragraph Counter is free to use, with no subscription required, subscriptions, or limitations on how often you can use it or the amount of text you can analyze. Enjoy repeated access to its features.

Does the tool save or store my text?add

No, we prioritize your privacy. The Paragraph Counter processes all text directly in your web browser. Your content is not uploaded to our server, uploaded to our server. Once you close the tab or refresh the page, your text is gone.
