About VAT/Tax Calculator

Introducing the Ultimate VAT/Tax Calculator

Navigating the complexities of Value Added Tax (VAT) or sales tax can be a headache for businesses and individuals alike. Whether you're a freelancer invoicing clients, a small business owner pricing products, or simply a shopper trying to understand the final cost, our Free VAT/Tax Calculator is designed to simplify your financial calculations. This intuitive online tool allows you to effortlessly add or remove VAT/Tax amounts from any price, ensuring accuracy and saving you valuable time.

How to Use Our Free VAT/Tax Calculator

Our VAT/Tax Calculator is designed for simplicity, making it accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial expertise. Follow these easy steps to get instant, accurate results:

  • Step 1: Enter Your Price: Input the original price you wish to calculate VAT for. This can be the net price (excluding VAT) or the gross price (including VAT).
  • Step 2: Select/Enter VAT Rate: Choose a standard VAT rate from our presets (e.g., 20%, 5%, 0%) or enter a custom percentage if your tax rate is unique.
  • Step 3: Choose Your Action: Decide whether you want to "Add VAT to Price" to find the gross total, or "Remove VAT from Price" to find the net amount and the VAT component.
  • Step 4: Get Instant Results: Our calculator will immediately display the new price, the VAT amount, and the original price, all broken down clearly for your understanding.

Frequently asked questions

What is VAT/Tax and why is it important?add

VAT (Value Added Tax) or sales tax is a consumption tax applied to goods and services. It's crucial for businesses to calculate it correctly for pricing, invoicing, and compliance, while individuals use it to understand the final cost of purchases.

How do I use this calculator to add VAT to a price?add

To add VAT, simply enter your net price (price before tax), select the applicable VAT rate, and choose the 'Add VAT' option. The calculator will instantly display the gross price (price including VAT) and the VAT amount.

How do I use this calculator to remove VAT from a price?add

To remove VAT, input the gross price (price including tax), select the correct VAT rate, and choose the 'Remove VAT' option. The tool will then calculate the original net price and the exact VAT amount that was included.
