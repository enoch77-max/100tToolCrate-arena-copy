About Tel Link Generator

Effortlessly Create Clickable Phone Links for Your Website

In today's mobile-first world, making it easy for your website visitors to contact you is paramount. Our free Tel Link Generator is a powerful, user-friendly tool designed to help you create instant, clickable telephone links (tel: links) for your website, emails, or digital documents. No more frustrating copy-pasting for your users – just one tap and they're connected!

A clickable phone number, also known as a 'call link,' transforms a static string of digits into an interactive element. When a user clicks or taps on it, their device automatically prompts them to make a call to that number. This seamless interaction significantly enhances user experience, especially for those browsing on smartphones or tablets. Stop losing potential customers due to inaccessible contact information and start converting more visitors into callers with our intuitive Tel Link Generator.

How to Use Our Free Tel Link Generator

Our Tel Link Generator simplifies the process of creating professional call links and accompanying QR codes into just a few quick steps. You don't need any coding knowledge; the tool handles all the technical details for you.

Step 1: Enter Your Phone Number

Simply type or paste the desired phone number into the input field. For best results and international compatibility, we recommend including your country code (e.g., +1 for the USA, +44 for the UK). Our generator is designed to work with various formats, but E.164 compliance is always a good practice.

Step 2: Generate Your Tel Link & QR Code

Once your number is entered, click the 'Generate' button. Instantly, our tool will create the correct HTML <a href="tel:"> tag for your phone number. Alongside this, it will automatically generate a unique QR code. This QR code, when scanned, will also initiate a call to the specified number, providing an additional layer of accessibility and convenience for your users.

Step 3: Copy & Paste

Your clickable phone link and the option to download the QR code will appear. Simply copy the generated HTML snippet and paste it directly into your website's HTML code where you want the phone number to appear. You can also right-click or use the download option to save your QR code image for print materials, business cards, or other digital assets. It's that easy to make your contact information truly interactive!

Frequently asked questions

What is a "tel:" link and why is it important for my website?add

A "tel:" link is an HTML attribute that makes a phone number clickable. When a user taps or clicks this link, their device (smartphone, tablet, or computer with calling software) is prompted to dial the number. It's crucial for websites because it dramatically improves user experience, especially on mobile devices, by removing the need to manually copy and paste phone numbers, thus making it easier for potential customers to contact you.

How do I use the Tel Link Generator to create a clickable phone number?add

Using our Tel Link Generator is simple: 1) Enter your full phone number, including the country code (e.g., +1 for USA, +44 for UK). 2) Click the 'Generate' button. 3) The tool will instantly provide you with the HTML code for your clickable link and a corresponding QR code. Simply copy the HTML and paste it into your website's code where you want the number to appear.

Does the tool support international phone numbers and QR codes?add

Yes, absolutely! Our Tel Link Generator is designed to support international phone numbers, ideally formatted according to the E.164 standard (e.g., +[country code][area code][local number]). It also automatically generates a scannable QR code for every number, which when scanned by a smartphone, will initiate a call to that exact international number, making your contact information accessible globally through multiple channels.

Quick tips

  • Test the generated QR code with your phone camera before printing.
  • Keep good contrast between the code and background for reliable scans.
  • Shorter links create simpler, easier-to-scan codes.
