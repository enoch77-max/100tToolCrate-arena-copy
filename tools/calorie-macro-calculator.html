About Calorie & Macro Calculator

Calorie & Macro Calculator: Build a Daily Target

This calculator estimates your basal metabolic rate (BMR), maintenance calories (TDEE) and a goal-adjusted daily calorie target. It then splits that target into protein, carbohydrate and fat grams using a macro ratio you choose. Enter your sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal in metric or imperial units.

The estimate uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, one of the most widely used BMR formulas, multiplied by an activity factor. Real energy needs vary with genetics, training history, sleep, medication and how accurately portions are measured, so treat the result as a starting point and adjust based on two to four weeks of real-world results.

Safety and Accuracy

Very low calorie targets can be unsafe without supervision. The tool warns when a target falls below common minimums, but it cannot account for medical conditions, pregnancy or eating disorders. Speak to a registered dietitian or clinician before making large dietary changes.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

How are daily calories calculated?add

The tool uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate BMR, multiplies it by an activity factor to estimate maintenance calories, then applies the surplus or deficit for your selected goal. One kilogram of body weight is roughly equivalent to 7,700 kcal, so a 0.5 kg weekly loss implies about a 500 kcal daily deficit.

Which macro split should I choose?add

Balanced (30% protein, 40% carbs, 30% fat) is a reasonable default for most people. High protein can help with satiety and muscle retention during a deficit, lower carb can suit preference or blood-sugar management, and lower fat can help with high-volume eating. The best split is one you can sustain.

Is this calculator medical advice?add

No. It is an educational estimate, not a prescription. Medical conditions, medication, pregnancy and a history of disordered eating all change what is appropriate. Consult a qualified professional for personalised advice.

Why is my calorie target below 1,200 or 1,500 kcal?add

That usually happens with a small body size, a low activity level or an aggressive deficit. The tool shows a warning because very low intakes can make it hard to meet nutrient needs. Choose a smaller deficit or seek professional guidance.
