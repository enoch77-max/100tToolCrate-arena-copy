How to Use Our Free Online Blur Tool

Blurring parts of your image has never been simpler. Follow these straightforward steps to hide sensitive information quickly:

  • Upload Your Image: Click the "Upload Image" button or drag and drop your photo directly into the designated area. Our tool supports popular image formats like JPG, PNG, and GIF.

  • Adjust Blur Intensity (Optional): Our tool typically provides an option to adjust the blur strength. Use a slider to increase or decrease the intensity of the blur effect until it meets your requirements.
  • Review and Download: Preview your blurred image to ensure all sensitive information is adequately hidden. Once satisfied, click the "Download" button to save your new, privacy-protected image to your device.

No account registration, no software downloads – just quick, secure image blurring online!
