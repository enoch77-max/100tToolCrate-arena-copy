Our PDF Watermark Tool is designed for simplicity. You can add a text watermark to your PDF in just a few straightforward steps:

Frequently asked questions

What is a PDF watermark and why should I use one?add

A PDF watermark is a visible, often semi-transparent, text or image overlay that appears across the pages of a PDF document. It's commonly used to protect intellectual property, indicate the status of a document (e.g., "Draft," "Confidential"), or brand documents with a company logo or name.

Is your PDF Watermark Tool free to use and secure?add

Yes, our PDF Watermark Tool is completely free with no subscription required or subscriptions. Your security and privacy are paramount; selected files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.

Can I customize the appearance of my text watermark?add

Absolutely! Our tool offers extensive customization options. You can specify the exact text for your watermark, choose from various fonts, adjust the text size and color, control the opacity (transparency), set the rotation angle, and even define its position on the page to perfectly suit your needs.
