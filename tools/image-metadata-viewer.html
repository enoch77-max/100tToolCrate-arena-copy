About Image Metadata Viewer

Unlock Hidden Insights with Our Free Online Image Metadata Viewer

Every digital image carries a wealth of information beyond what meets the eye. From the camera model that captured the shot to the exact date and time it was taken, and even precise GPS coordinates, this data is embedded as 'metadata' within the image file itself. Our free Image Metadata Viewer is an indispensable online tool designed to effortlessly extract and display this hidden information, giving you a deeper understanding of any photograph.

Metadata, often referred to as EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format), IPTC, or XMP data, provides crucial context. Whether you're a professional photographer analyzing your work, a curious enthusiast, or someone needing to verify details about an image, our viewer makes accessing this data simple and straightforward.

How to Use Our Online EXIF Viewer

Accessing the metadata of your images has never been easier. Our Image Metadata Viewer is designed for simplicity, requiring no software download or technical expertise. Follow these quick steps:

  • Step 1: Upload Your Image – Click the 'Upload Image' button and select the image file from your computer or device. We support popular formats like JPEG, PNG, and TIFF.
  • Step 2: Instant Analysis – Once uploaded, our tool automatically processes the image and extracts all available metadata within seconds.
  • Step 3: View the Details – All the extracted EXIF and metadata will be displayed in an organized, easy-to-read format directly in your browser.

Key Features of Our Image Metadata Viewer

Our online tool provides a comprehensive breakdown of your image's hidden data, presenting it clearly and concisely. Here are some of the critical details you can expect to uncover:

  • Camera & Lens Information: Discover the camera make and model, specific lens used, focal length, aperture (f-number), shutter speed, and ISO speed rating.
  • Date & Time: See the exact date and time the photo was taken, often including the original creation and last modification dates.
  • Location Data (GPS): If the camera or device had GPS enabled, view the latitude, longitude, and altitude where the image was captured.
  • Image Properties: Access details like image dimensions (width, height), resolution, and file size.
  • Exposure Settings: Get insights into exposure bias, metering mode, flash status, and white balance settings.
  • Software & Copyright: Identify the software used to process the image and any embedded copyright information.

Frequently asked questions

What is image metadata?add

Image metadata is data embedded within an image file that provides information about the image. This can include technical details like camera model, lens, exposure settings (aperture, shutter speed, ISO), date and time the photo was taken, and even GPS location if enabled. It can also include copyright information or software used for editing.

Is the Image Metadata Viewer free to use?add

Yes, our Image Metadata Viewer is completely free to use. There are no subscription required, subscriptions, or software installations required. You can access it anytime, anywhere, directly from your web browser.

What types of image files does this tool support?add

Our online tool primarily supports popular image formats such as JPEG, PNG, and TIFF. While EXIF data is most commonly found in JPEG files, the viewer will extract any available metadata from the supported formats you upload.

  • Crop and resize first, then compress the final export and inspect its quality.
