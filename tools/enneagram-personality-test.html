About Enneagram Test

Free Enneagram Personality Test

The Enneagram maps nine interconnected personality types, each defined by a core motivation, a deepest fear, and a habitual way of coping with the world. Unlike many personality quizzes, it focuses less on what you do and more on why you do it - making it especially useful for self-awareness, conflict resolution, and teamwork.

The Nine Enneagram Types

  • Type 1 - The Reformer: principled, purposeful, self-controlled, perfectionistic.
  • Type 2 - The Helper: generous, people-pleasing, warm, driven to be needed.
  • Type 3 - The Achiever: ambitious, adaptive, image-conscious, relentlessly productive.
  • Type 4 - The Individualist: sensitive, expressive, introspective, drawn to depth and meaning.
  • Type 5 - The Investigator: cerebral, private, observant, protecting energy and knowledge.
  • Type 6 - The Loyalist: committed, security-seeking, vigilant, worst-case planner.
  • Type 7 - The Enthusiast: spontaneous, optimistic, variety-seeking, reframing pain as possibility.
  • Type 8 - The Challenger: direct, decisive, protective, allergic to control by others.
  • Type 9 - The Peacemaker: easygoing, agreeable, conflict-avoiding, seeking inner and outer harmony.

How Your Result Is Calculated

The 45 statements in this test cover five items per type. After you answer, each type earns a raw score between 5 and 25, normalized to a 0-100 balance. The highest-scoring type is your dominant type, and the stronger of its two adjacent types on the Enneagram circle is shown as your wing (for example, a Type 5 wing 4, or 5w4). Runners-up are shown so you can see the full landscape of your motivations.

Use It Well

The Enneagram is a mirror, not a cage. Your dominant type describes your automatic patterns under stress and in comfort - growth happens by becoming aware of those patterns, not by being defined by them. For best results, answer quickly and honestly, and read the descriptions of your top two or three types rather than only the winner.

Private by Design

This test runs entirely in your browser. No answers are uploaded, stored, or shared, and you can retake it as often as you like with zero friction - no account, no email, no paywall.

Accuracy & limitations

This is an educational self-report exercise, not a validated clinical diagnosis. Do not use the result for medical, employment or other high-impact decisions.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a free Enneagram test?add

Online Enneagram tests are self-report screeners. They reliably surface your likely dominant type if you answer honestly, but the Enneagram itself is a typology rather than a clinical scale - treat the result as a strong hypothesis to explore, not a diagnosis.

What is an Enneagram wing?add

Your wing is the adjacent type that most flavors your dominant type. A Type 9 with a Type 8 wing (9w8) is more assertive than a 9w1, who is more principled. Wings add nuance without changing your core motivation.

Can my Enneagram type change?add

Your core type is generally stable, but your access to the healthier and less healthy expressions of every type shifts with stress, security, and personal growth. Many people type themselves differently after 30 because they have outgrown their autopilot patterns.

Are my answers stored or sent to a server?add

No. Scoring happens locally in your browser with plain JavaScript. Nothing leaves your device, and no results are saved after you close the page.

Which Enneagram type is the best?add

None. Every type has healthy, average, and stressed expressions. The point of the Enneagram is understanding your pattern so you can choose it consciously instead of being run by it unconsciously.

Quick tips

  • Answer honestly and quickly - your first instinct is usually the most accurate.
  • There are no right or wrong answers, only useful patterns.
