Frequently asked questions

What is a Date Difference Calculator?add

A Date Difference Calculator is an online tool that quickly determines the exact number of days, weeks, or even months and years between any two selected dates. It automates what would otherwise be a tedious manual counting process, providing accurate results instantly.

How do I calculate the days between two dates using this tool?add

Simply select your 'Start Date' using the first calendar input and your 'End Date' using the second. Once both dates are chosen, click the 'Calculate' button, and the total number of days between them will be displayed immediately.

Does the calculator include the start or end date in the total count?add

Our calculator typically calculates the number of full days *between* the two selected dates. This means it counts the full 24-hour periods from the end of the start date until the beginning of the end date. For example, the difference between January 1 and January 2 is 1 day.
