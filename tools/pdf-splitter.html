About PDF Splitter

Free Online PDF Splitter: Extract Pages with Ease

Are you looking to break down large PDF documents into smaller, more manageable files? Our free online PDF Splitter tool is designed to do just that, quickly and efficiently. Whether you need to extract specific pages, separate chapters, or simply organize your documents better, our PDF splitter provides a straightforward solution without the need for complex software installations.

This powerful web-based tool allows you to select and extract any range of pages from your original PDF, creating a brand-new PDF file containing only the content you need. Say goodbye to cumbersome manual work and hello to streamlined document management.

How to Use Our PDF Splitter Tool

Splitting your PDF files has never been simpler. Follow these easy steps to extract pages and create new PDFs in moments:

  1. Upload Your PDF: Click the "Upload PDF" button or drag and drop your PDF file into the designated area.
  2. Select Pages to Extract: Once your PDF is loaded, you'll see options to specify which pages you want to split. You can choose individual pages (e.g., "1, 5, 10"), a range of pages (e.g., "2-7"), or even split every page into separate PDFs.
  3. Split and Preview: After defining your selection, click the "Split PDF" button. Our tool will process your request instantly.
  4. Download Your New PDF: Your newly created PDF file(s) will be ready for download. You can save them directly to your device.

Frequently asked questions

What is a PDF Splitter?add

A PDF splitter is an online tool that allows you to extract specific pages or ranges of pages from a larger PDF document, creating one or more new PDF files containing only the selected content. It's ideal for breaking down large documents into smaller, more manageable parts.

How do I split a PDF file using this tool?add

Splitting a PDF is easy! Simply upload your PDF document, then select the pages or page ranges you wish to extract (e.g., "1, 3-5, 10"). After making your selection, click the "Split PDF" button, and your newly created PDF document(s) will be ready for instant download.

Is it safe to use an online PDF splitter for my documents?add

Yes, your document security and privacy are our top priorities. Selected PDFs are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.
