About 2×2 Passport Photo Cropper

A single, explicit output preset

This tool center-crops the largest square from your source and exports a 600×600-pixel JPEG on a white canvas. That corresponds to 2×2 inches at 300 DPI. It does not currently provide presets for every country or document type.

Before using the result

Confirm that your issuing authority asks for this exact size and permits the crop. Center cropping may remove too much of the head, shoulders or background; use the general image cropper when you need to choose a different region.

Official acceptance also depends on recency, pose, lighting, expression, head proportions, background and alteration rules. A correctly sized export is not a compliance certificate.

Accuracy & limitations

The only current preset center-crops to a 600×600 JPEG for 2×2 inches at 300 DPI. It does not support every country's dimensions or certify acceptance. Verify recency, pose, lighting, background, head proportions and alteration rules with the issuing authority.
