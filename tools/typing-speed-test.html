About Typing Speed Test

Typing Speed Test: Measure WPM and Accuracy

Choose a 15, 30, 60 or 120-second test and start typing the passage. The timer begins with your first keystroke, and live statistics show net words per minute, accuracy and the number of incorrect characters. When the time runs out, you get a final result with net WPM, raw WPM, accuracy and characters typed.

Net WPM is calculated as correct characters divided by five, divided by elapsed minutes. Accuracy is the share of typed characters that match the target passage. Paste and drop are disabled so the result reflects actual typing rather than copied text. The test uses a randomised set of common English words and runs entirely in your browser.

What Is a Good Typing Speed?

Around 40 WPM is a common working speed, 60 WPM is comfortable for most professional roles and 80 WPM or more is fast. Accuracy matters as much as raw speed: a fast result with many errors creates more correction work. Practise with relaxed hands, look at the screen rather than the keyboard and aim for steady rhythm.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?add

Net WPM uses correct characters only: correct characters divided by five, then divided by the elapsed time in minutes. Raw WPM uses every typed character. This is why accuracy has a direct effect on your net score.

Why is paste disabled?add

Pasting would bypass typing and make the result meaningless. The test blocks paste and drag-and-drop so the score reflects your actual keystrokes.

What is a good typing speed?add

About 40 WPM is a typical working speed, 60 WPM is comfortable for most office roles and 80 WPM or above is fast. Accuracy above 95% is usually more valuable than a small gain in raw speed.

Does the test work on phones and tablets?add

Yes, but an on-screen keyboard behaves differently from a physical keyboard and usually produces lower scores. For a comparable result, use a desktop or laptop with a physical keyboard.
