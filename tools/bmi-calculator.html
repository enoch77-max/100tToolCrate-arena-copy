About BMI Calculator

BMI Calculator: Understand Your Body Mass Index

Body mass index (BMI) is a quick screening measure that compares your weight with your height. This calculator shows your BMI, the standard adult category, a healthy weight range for your height and your BMI prime. Switch between metric and imperial units at any time; the result updates as you type.

BMI is useful for spotting broad weight trends across populations, but it is not a diagnosis. Muscle mass, body composition, pregnancy, age and ethnicity can all affect how a result should be interpreted. Use the number as a starting point for a conversation with a qualified clinician rather than as a target on its own.

How the Result Is Calculated

The calculator divides your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in metres. Adult categories are under 18.5 (underweight), 18.5 to 24.9 (normal weight), 25 to 29.9 (overweight) and 30 or above (obesity). Everything runs in your browser, so your measurements are never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?add

No. BMI is a population-level screening tool, not a direct measure of body fat or health. Athletes with high muscle mass can score as overweight, while older adults or people with low muscle mass can score as normal despite excess body fat. Pregnancy, children and some ethnic groups need different assessment methods.

What is a healthy BMI range for adults?add

The World Health Organization uses 18.5 to 24.9 as the normal-weight range for adults. A result outside that range is not automatically a health problem, but it is a signal to look at the wider picture with a clinician.

Does this BMI calculator work in imperial units?add

Yes. Switch to Imperial to enter height in feet and inches and weight in pounds. The calculator converts the values internally and shows the healthy weight range in pounds.

Are BMI thresholds the same for men and women?add

The standard adult thresholds are the same, but body composition differs between men and women. BMI should therefore be interpreted alongside waist circumference, medical history and other health measures.
