About Text to Speech

Text to Speech: Listen to Any Text in Your Browser

Paste an article, study note, script or document and let your browser read it aloud. Choose from the voices installed on your device, then adjust speed, pitch and volume to suit the content. Playback can be paused, resumed and stopped at any time.

The tool uses the Web Speech API, so the text is processed by your browser’s speech engine rather than uploaded to a server. Voice quality and availability depend on the operating system and browser; some voices are generated locally, while others may use a cloud service provided by the browser or OS vendor.

Practical Uses

Text to speech is useful for proofreading, accessibility, language practice, hands-free reading and turning short scripts into rough audio drafts. It is not a replacement for professional narration, and the tool does not record or export audio files. For long documents, split the text into sections so you can pause and resume more easily.

Accuracy & limitations

Voices, languages and speech behavior depend on the browser and operating system. Audio export is available only where the implementation explicitly supports it; a voice preview is not a generated audio file.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?add

The page passes your text to the browser’s speech synthesis engine and does not send it to 100ToolCrate. Depending on the voice selected, the browser or operating system may use a cloud voice service under its own privacy policy. Local voices keep everything on your device.

Can I download the generated audio?add

This tool does not record or export audio. It plays speech through your device’s audio output. If you need a downloadable file, use a dedicated recorder or a text-to-speech service that supports audio export.

Why do I see different voices on another device?add

Voices are supplied by the operating system and browser, not by this website. A Windows laptop, an iPhone and a Linux desktop will usually expose different voice lists and languages.

Does text to speech work offline?add

It can, if your browser and operating system provide local voices. Some voices require an internet connection because they are generated by a cloud service. Test the selected voice before relying on it offline.

Quick tips

  • Paste directly into the editor - counts update as you type.
  • Use copy or download to move the result into your own workflow.
  • Clear the editor between unrelated documents to avoid mixing data.
