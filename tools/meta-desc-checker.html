Frequently asked questions

What is a meta description and why is it important for SEO?add

A meta description is an HTML tag that provides a brief summary of a web page's content. While not a direct ranking factor, a well-written, relevant meta description dramatically influences a user's decision to click on your search result (CTR). Higher CTR signals relevance to search engines, indirectly boosting your SEO.

What are the ideal length limits for meta descriptions?add

Google typically truncates meta descriptions around 150-160 characters on desktop and slightly less on mobile (around 120-130 characters). However, Google measures by pixel width, not just character count. It's generally recommended to keep your core message within 120-130 characters to ensure it's fully visible across devices, while allowing for up to 155-160 characters if every character counts.

Will Google always use my provided meta description?add

Not always. Google may sometimes choose to generate its own meta description based on the query and the content of your page if it believes its version is more relevant to the user. However, providing a compelling, optimized meta description significantly increases the chances of it being used and helps Google understand your page's context.
