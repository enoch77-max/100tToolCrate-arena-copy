What is the Case Converter Tool?

Our free online Case Converter is an indispensable utility designed to effortlessly transform your text between various capitalization styles. Whether you need to scream in all caps (UPPERCASE), whisper in all small letters (lowercase), or meticulously format a headline in Title Case, our tool handles it with precision and speed. Ideal for writers, programmers, students, and anyone dealing with text data, this converter streamlines the process of adjusting text cases, saving you valuable time and ensuring consistency.

Forget manual retyping or tedious word processor formatting. With a few clicks, you can convert paragraphs, sentences, or even single words into your desired case, making it perfect for preparing content for different platforms, coding, or academic writing.

Key Features of Our Online Case Converter

Explore the full range of text transformations available:

  • UPPERCASE: Converts all letters in your text to capital letters (e.g., "HELLO WORLD").
  • lowercase: Transforms all letters in your text to small letters (e.g., "hello world").
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each significant word, commonly used for titles and headlines (e.g., "Hello World").
  • Sentence Case: Capitalizes the first letter of the first word in each sentence and proper nouns, just like standard writing (e.g., "Hello world.").
  • Capitalize Word: Capitalizes the first letter of every single word, regardless of significance (e.g., "Hello World").
  • Alternating Case: Converts text into an alternating pattern of uppercase and lowercase letters (e.g., "hElLo WoRlD").
  • Toggle Case / Inverse Case: Flips the case of each letter (e.g., "hELLO wORLD" becomes "Hello World").
  • Kebab Case: Converts spaces to hyphens and text to lowercase (e.g., "hello-world"). Often used in CSS and URLs.
  • Snake Case: Converts spaces to underscores and text to lowercase (e.g., "hello_world"). Common in programming.
  • Pascal Case: Removes spaces and capitalizes the first letter of each word, typically used for class names in programming (e.g., "HelloWorld").
  • Camel Case: Removes spaces, capitalizes the first letter of each word except the very first one, common for variable names (e.g., "helloWorld").

What's the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?add

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every significant word in a phrase (e.g., "The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog"). Sentence case, on the other hand, only capitalizes the first letter of the first word in a sentence and proper nouns, similar to standard writing (e.g., "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.").
