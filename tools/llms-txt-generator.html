About llms.txt Generator

What Is llms.txt?

llms.txt is an emerging convention - like robots.txt for search engines - that gives large language models a clean, curated markdown map of your site. Instead of forcing an AI crawler to guess your information architecture, llms.txt tells it what your product is, which pages matter most, and where to find authoritative documentation. Sites that publish one are easier for AI answers, agents and IDE copilots to understand and cite accurately.

How to Use This Generator

  1. Site name and summary: one line that tells an LLM who you are and what you do - this becomes the H1 and the blockquote.
  2. Sections: group your most valuable pages - documentation, API references, pricing, about pages, policies, blog highlights - into named sections.
  3. Link entries: add a title, a URL, and an optional one-line description for each entry.
  4. Generate and publish: copy the output or download llms.txt and place it at https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt.

llms.txt vs robots.txt vs sitemap.xml

robots.txt controls what crawlers may access, sitemap.xml lists every indexable URL, and llms.txt is a human- and model-curated briefing: fewer pages, higher signal, written in markdown LLMs read natively. Use all three - they serve different readers. Pair this file with an AI Crawler robots.txt policy if you want to decide which AI bots may crawl at all.

Best Practices

  • Keep it under roughly 10,000 tokens - brevity is the entire point.
  • Link only canonical, stable pages; avoid filtered or paginated URLs.
  • Prefer descriptive link titles over raw URLs.
  • Re-generate it whenever your information architecture changes.
  • Verify it is reachable at /llms.txt without redirects or auth.

Accuracy & limitations

An llms.txt file is a convention, not a guarantee that models or crawlers will read, obey or cite it. Keep canonical robots.txt, metadata, sitemaps and public permissions aligned with the policy you actually want.

Frequently asked questions

Is llms.txt an official standard?add

It is a widely adopted community proposal published at llmstxt.org, not an IETF or W3C standard. Major AI products (Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT browsing, IDE copilots) increasingly honor it, and it is inexpensive to publish and maintain.

Where should the llms.txt file live?add

At the root of your domain - https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt - served as text/plain or text/markdown with no authentication or redirects. Some teams also publish an extended llms-full.txt with full documentation content.

Will llms.txt improve my Google rankings?add

Not directly - it is not a traditional ranking factor. Its payoff is in AI answer engines and coding assistants, where being accurately understood and cited drives referral traffic and brand visibility in AI-generated responses.

What should I include in llms.txt?add

Your product summary, then a handful of sections linking your documentation, API reference, pricing, about/contact pages and key blog posts - with one-line descriptions. Quality over quantity: 20 excellent links beat 200 mediocre ones.

Quick tips

  • Aim for a unique title and description on every page.
  • Preview how search and social platforms will display your content.
  • Use the generated markup as a starting point, then refine the copy.
