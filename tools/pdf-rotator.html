About PDF Page Rotator

What is the PDF Page Rotator?

Welcome to our free online PDF Page Rotator, the ultimate solution for fixing incorrectly oriented PDF documents. Have you ever downloaded a PDF only to find some pages are sideways or even upside down? Our intuitive tool is designed to solve this common problem effortlessly. It allows you to permanently rotate individual pages or entire documents to the correct orientation, ensuring your PDFs are always presented perfectly.

Whether it’s a scanned document, an eBook, or a report, our PDF Page Rotator provides a quick, secure, and highly effective way to adjust your document’s view. Say goodbye to craning your neck or constantly adjusting your screen – with just a few clicks, your PDF pages will be rotated and saved exactly how you need them.

How to Use the PDF Page Rotator Tool

Our PDF Page Rotator is designed for simplicity, making the process of correcting your document's orientation incredibly straightforward. Follow these easy steps to get your PDF pages rotated in no time:

  1. Upload Your PDF: Click on the "Upload PDF" button or drag and drop your PDF file into the designated area. Our tool will quickly process your document.
  2. Select Pages & Rotate: Once your PDF is uploaded, you'll see a preview of its pages. You can select specific pages to rotate or choose to apply rotation to all pages. Then, simply click the "Rotate Left" or "Rotate Right" button (or specify degrees like 90°, 180°, 270°) until the pages are in your desired orientation.
  3. Apply & Download: After making your adjustments, click the "Apply Changes" or "Rotate PDF" button. Our tool will process the rotations. Once complete, you can download your newly oriented PDF file directly to your device. The changes are permanent!

It's that simple! No complex software, no confusing menus – just a clear path to perfectly rotated PDF documents.
