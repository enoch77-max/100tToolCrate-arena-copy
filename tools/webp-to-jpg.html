What is WebP and Why Convert to JPG?

WebP is an image format developed by Google, known for providing superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web. It significantly reduces file sizes with configurable quality settings, leading to faster website loading times. However, despite its advantages, WebP images are not universally supported by all software, browsers, and older devices. This is where JPG comes in.

JPG (or JPEG) is one of the most common image formats, boasting almost universal compatibility. It's supported by virtually every image viewer, editor, web browser, and operating system. Converting your WebP files to JPG ensures that your images can be viewed, edited, and shared without any compatibility issues, making them perfect for printing, uploading to various social media platforms, or using in applications that don't yet support WebP.
