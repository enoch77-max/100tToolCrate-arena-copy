Frequently asked questions

What does it mean to 'flip' or 'mirror' an image?add

Flipping or mirroring an image refers to reversing its orientation. A horizontal flip (or mirror) reverses the image from left to right, like looking in a mirror. A vertical flip reverses it from top to bottom.

Is this Image Flip/Mirror tool completely free to use?add

Yes, absolutely! Our Image Flip/Mirror tool is free to use, with no subscription required, subscriptions, or watermarks on your processed images. You can use it as many times as you need.

What image file formats are supported by this tool?add

Our online tool supports a wide range of popular image formats, including JPEG/JPG, PNG, GIF, and BMP. Simply upload your image in one of these formats, and you can easily flip or mirror it.

Do I need to install any software to use this tool?add

No, you don't need to install any software or plugins. Our Image Flip/Mirror tool is entirely web-based, meaning you can access and use it directly from your browser on any device with an internet connection.

Is my image data secure and private when using this tool?add

Yes, we prioritize your privacy. Selected images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.

Quick tips
