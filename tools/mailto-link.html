About Mailto Link Generator

What is a Mailto Link Generator?

A Mailto Link Generator is a free online tool designed to simplify the creation of HTML mailto: links. These special links, when clicked, automatically open a user's default email client (like Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail, etc.) with pre-filled information. Instead of manually coding the complex syntax for email recipients, subjects, body text, and even CC/BCC fields, our generator handles all the technical details for you. It's an essential tool for website owners, bloggers, marketers, and anyone who needs to provide a convenient, one-click email contact option.

How to Use Our Mailto Link Generator

Creating your perfect mailto link is incredibly straightforward with our intuitive generator. Follow these simple steps:

  • Enter Recipient Email: Start by typing the primary email address(es) into the 'To' field. You can add multiple recipients separated by commas.
  • Add Subject Line (Optional): Fill in the 'Subject' field with a descriptive subject for the email. This helps users quickly understand the email's purpose.
  • Compose Email Body (Optional): Write the default message content in the 'Body' field. This text will appear pre-filled in the user's email client.
  • Include CC & BCC (Optional): If you need to include additional recipients for carbon copy (CC) or blind carbon copy (BCC), simply enter their email addresses in the respective fields, separated by commas.
  • Generate & Copy: Once all your desired fields are filled, click the "Generate Mailto Link" button. The tool will instantly create the HTML code for your mailto link. You can then easily copy this code to your clipboard and paste it directly into your website's HTML, blog posts, or any other digital content.
