About PDF Compressor

Compress after finishing document edits

Merge, reorder, rotate or extract pages first, then compress the finished document once. This avoids repeatedly degrading page images and gives you a clear original for comparison.

Why savings vary

Documents dominated by large images may offer more opportunity for reduction than files that are already optimized. Some PDFs can shrink very little. Output size and visual quality depend on the source and the browser-side processing method; there is no universal compression ratio.

Quality checks that matter

  • Inspect small text, stamps, signatures and QR codes at 100% zoom.
  • Confirm that every expected page opens and remains readable.
  • Keep the original until the recipient or upload portal accepts the result.

Accuracy & limitations

Compression cannot guarantee a target size or unchanged visual quality. Already optimized PDFs may shrink little, and aggressive image recompression can blur scans, small text, signatures or QR codes.
