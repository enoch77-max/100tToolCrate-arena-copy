About Color Picker from Image

What is the Color Picker from Image Tool?

Our free online Color Picker from Image tool is an essential utility for designers, developers, artists, and anyone needing precise color information. It allows you to effortlessly upload any image and then pinpoint and extract the exact color from any pixel within that image. Whether you're trying to match a specific brand color, recreate a design element, or simply identify a fascinating hue, this tool provides accurate Hex, RGB, and HSL color codes with just a click.

Forget the guesswork and manual color matching. Our user-friendly application simplifies the process, making professional color extraction accessible to everyone. It's an indispensable resource for maintaining consistency in your visual projects, ensuring your colors are always on point.

How to Use Our Online Image Color Picker

Using our Color Picker from Image tool is incredibly straightforward. You don't need any prior technical knowledge or special software. Just follow these simple steps:

  • Upload Your Image: Click the "Upload Image" button and select the image file (JPG, PNG, GIF, etc.) from your device. Your image will instantly appear in the browser.
  • Click to Select Color: Once your image is loaded, simply move your mouse cursor over any part of the image. You'll see a magnifying glass or a crosshair that helps you precisely locate your desired pixel. Click on the specific area whose color you wish to extract.
  • Get Instant Color Codes: Immediately after clicking, the tool will display the exact color codes – including Hexadecimal (Hex), RGB (Red, Green, Blue), and HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) – for the selected pixel.
  • Copy and Use: Easily copy the desired color code to your clipboard with a single click. You can then paste it into your design software, CSS code, or any application you're using.
