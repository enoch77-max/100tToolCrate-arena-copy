How to Use Our PDF Signature Tool

Signing your PDF documents has never been easier. Follow these simple steps to add your signature in just a few clicks:

1. Upload Your PDF Document

Begin by clicking the "Upload PDF" button or dragging and dropping your PDF file directly into the designated area. Our tool quickly processes your document, preparing it for signature application.

2. Create Your Signature

Once your PDF is loaded, you'll be prompted to create your signature. You have several convenient options:

  • Type: Simply type your name, and choose from various elegant fonts to generate a professional-looking digital signature.
  • Draw: Use your mouse or trackpad to draw your signature directly onto the screen, providing a personalized and authentic feel.
  • Upload Image: If you already have a scanned image of your signature, you can upload it and place it onto your document.

3. Place and Adjust Your Signature

After creating your signature, it will appear on your PDF. You can then effortlessly drag and drop it to the desired position on the document. Resize, rotate, or delete the signature if needed, ensuring it fits perfectly within your document's layout.

4. Download Your Signed PDF

Once you are satisfied with your signature placement, simply click the "Download" button. Your newly signed PDF document will be instantly available for download, ready to be saved, shared, or printed.
