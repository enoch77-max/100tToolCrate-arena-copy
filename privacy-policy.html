Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-11-10

At 100ToolCrate (accessible from https://100toolcrate.com), the privacy of our visitors is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by 100ToolCrate and how we protect your personal data.

1. Browser Processing, Public Execution and Service Data

Supported browser tools process selected files, images, text and documents locally using browser APIs and client-side libraries. Library code may be downloaded from listed CDNs, but the selected tool input is not sent to our application server by those browser tools. The separate public REST/MCP execution API processes only the selected text, JSON, time or calculation inputs listed in its catalog; execution inputs and outputs are transient and are not persisted by that endpoint. Standard hosting logs and rate-limit infrastructure still apply.

Choose the appropriate data path: Use browser tools when you need local file processing. Do not send secrets or unnecessary personal data to the execution API. Contact, sponsorship and service forms use FormSubmit. Paid-service files are not local-only and are transferred only after a written scope and approved channel are agreed.

2. Log Files & Server Metrics

100ToolCrate follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting services do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like any other website, 100ToolCrate uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences (such as Light Mode / Dark Mode state) and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

On the production site, optional advertising is provided by Google AdSense. The Google advertising script is not loaded on preview hosts and is not loaded until you choose Allow ads. Choosing Essential only keeps the tools available without loading AdSense. Your choice is stored in local browser storage under tc-ad-consent-v1; workflow checklist progress is stored separately under tc-workflow-progress-v1. You can revisit this choice using in the footer or here.

When enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Google's advertising privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Availability of ads depends on account approval, regional rules, browser settings and Google policies.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on 100ToolCrate, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

5. Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), consumers have specific data privacy rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: Request disclosure of collected personal data categories and specific pieces of data.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Request deletion of any personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Request that a business does not sell or share the consumer's personal data.

100ToolCrate does not require tool user accounts or maintain a database of browser-tool file contents. SQLite stores anonymous aggregate tool counters, not visitor identifiers. Contact and service messages are delivered through FormSubmit and may exist in the operator's email. For a privacy inquiry, contact privacy@100toolcrate.com.

6. Children's Information (Children's Privacy)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. 100ToolCrate does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Form Processing

When you submit a contact, advertising or service form, the details you enter are transmitted to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form delivery service, and forwarded to support@100toolcrate.com. The operator may retain correspondence in email and service records for response, delivery, accounting and abuse-prevention purposes. We do not sell these submissions. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal data; you can email directly instead.
